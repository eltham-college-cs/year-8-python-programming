## The big idea

Three things to remember from the first two units:

- **Comma or plus.** A comma in `print()` adds a space and accepts any type. A plus glues text together with no space, and refuses to mix text and numbers.
- **`input()` always gives back a string**, even if the user types `12`.
- **Casting** changes the type: `int()` turns text into a number for maths; `str()` turns a number into text for joining with `+`.

<Note title="The 1212 surprise">
If `age` holds the text `"12"`, then `age * 2` gives `1212`. Multiplying text repeats it. `int(age) * 2` gives `24`.
</Note>
